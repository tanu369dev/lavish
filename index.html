How a sale runs

The same three steps for buyer and seller.

Step 1

List

The seller adds photos, odometer reading, service history and an asking price.

Step 2

Check

Registration, insurance and chassis details are reviewed before the listing shows a documents badge.

Step 3

Hand over

The buyer reserves with a refundable deposit, inspects the bike, then pays the balance at handover.
